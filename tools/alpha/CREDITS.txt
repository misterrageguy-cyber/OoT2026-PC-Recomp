Ocarina Reborn - source and credits

Built on Hookshot by NotRexed and the Hookshot contributors.
Original project: https://github.com/NotRexed/Hookshot
Engine base: Hookshot v2.2.0-rc.1 (GPL-3.0-or-later).

Hookshot provides the recompilation engine, console runtime, Vulkan renderer
and game-build pipeline. Ocarina Reborn adds its own launcher, installer,
presets, diagnostics, interface changes and custom gameplay hooks.
Ocarina Reborn is independently maintained, not an official Hookshot release.
