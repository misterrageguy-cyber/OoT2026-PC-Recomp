# Ocarina Reborn v1.0.7

## Launcher and loading screens

- Restored the Ocarina artwork and animated Navi in the launcher.
- Restored the previous artwork on the build screen and the game's shader/pipeline loading screen.

## Website

- The page and preview title is now **Ocarina Reborn**.
- Updated the search/social preview and favicon to the supplied blue Ocarina. Search engines refresh their cached previews separately.

## Updating

Restart your launcher and choose **Update**, then install over your existing installation. Afterwards, allow the launcher to update the game components so the loading-screen logo is applied. No uninstall or game-file reimport is required; saves, settings and your local voice mods stay.

Voice recordings and launcher music are not included. This release changes the presentation; it adds no new gameplay or performance fixes.
