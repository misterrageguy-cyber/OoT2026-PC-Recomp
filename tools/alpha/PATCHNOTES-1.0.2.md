**Ocarina Reborn v1.0.2 — Big Fix Update + Classic Link Voices** 💙

🛠️ **Fixes**
- 🎮 **Controllers on new installs:** PlayStation, Switch Pro and Steam controllers work again. Before, only Xbox worked and the game seemed stuck on the title screen.
- 🧠 **RAM guard fixed:** no more texture reloading over and over (hitches of up to 0.9 s).
- 🖥️ **8 GB cards:** compact video memory now turns on automatically, against freezes of up to 1.7 s in new areas.
- ⬇️ **Update download** no longer fails with "Permission denied".
- 💥 **Launcher** no longer crashes when you click, move or resize it during a build or update.

🎙️ **Classic N64 voices (optional)**
- Young Link and Adult Link each get their own setting in Settings > Audio: Default or Classic (N64).
- Import your own OOT_YoungLink.zip / OOT_AdultLink.zip → Classic (N64) → Save → restart. Voice packs are not included.

✨ **New options**
- 🌟 **Bloom** (Off / Low / Medium / High): soft glow around sun, fire and magic.
- 💀 **Death counter** (F1 > Gameplay).
- 📐 **Auto resolution:** FPS target 30 / 45 / 60 (Settings > Memory).
- ⚡ **Fast pipelines (test):** new scenes show at once while the full pipeline builds in the background.

🕹️ **Launcher**
- 🎮 **Controller support:** stick/D-pad, A select, B back, Start to play, LB/RB tabs.
- 🩺 **Diagnosis** now also warns about the "Balanced" power plan and 16 GB RAM or less.

💡 **Tip**
- 🔧 **6-core CPUs:** short hitches when loading new areas come from the CPU. Close background apps like Virtual Desktop.

🗣️ Voice issues: tell us which Link, which action and where. Errors: debug package in #logs.
