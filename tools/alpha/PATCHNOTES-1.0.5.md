# Ocarina Reborn v1.0.5 — Custom tunic colors

**🎨 Choose your tunic color**

- Pick your own color for Young Link and Adult Link's regular, Goron and Zora tunics.
- **Launcher → Settings → Tunic:** color picker, RGB values, HEX entry and a preview.
- **In-game F1 → Gameplay:** enable the custom color and adjust the red, green and blue sliders.
- Both menus use the same settings. Your color also works when starting the game directly.
- Choose **Original** to restore the original tunic colors. No separate downloads or manual mod installation are needed.

**🔄 Applying colors and updating**

- Save your changes and **restart the game** to apply a color or return to Original.
- Install this update over your existing installation, then let the launcher update the game components. **No clean reinstall or game-file reimport is required.** Your saves and settings stay.
- Colors affect the in-game tunics; inventory icons and prerecorded movies keep their original appearance.

Thanks for testing and sharing your feedback! 💙
